About

I'm an MSc student in Artificial Intelligence at Mila / Université de Montréal, working with Dr. Karim Jerbi's Cognitive & Computational Neuroscience Lab on predicting epilepsy from EEG recordings.

Before this, I was a software engineer at DoorDash, shipping UI features and running experiments. After that, my research path oscillated — an HCI lab studying trust in visualizations, then a brain aging lab at Ohio State analyzing data. That work left me curious about the AI tools, which is what led me to pursue my Master's in AI. Now, my project evaluates different machine learning methods in a clinical context, benchmarking how well they predict epilepsy from EEG data.
